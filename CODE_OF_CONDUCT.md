# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Critique code and ideas without personal attacks. Welcome people with different experience levels and backgrounds. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or share credentials or others' private data.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban repeated or serious violations. Decisions should consider context, impact and proportionality. Report harmful public content through GitHub's reporting tools; use private security reporting when sensitive information or credentials are involved. Do not reproduce private information in a public report.

This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other officially designated community spaces. It is a project-specific policy, not an assertion of affiliation with another conduct program.
